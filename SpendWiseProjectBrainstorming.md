SpendWise – Personal Expense Tracking Application
SpendWise is a personal expense-tracking application that helps people understand where their money goes, how much they spend, and which categories consume most of their money.
The main purpose is to make expense tracking simple and reduce the need for manual record-keeping.
1. What problem are we solving?
Nowadays, people make multiple digital payments every day using applications like Google Pay, PhonePe and Paytm.
For example, imagine your daily expenses:
₹80 for an auto-rickshaw.
₹150 for lunch.
₹40 for tea and snacks.
₹300 for groceries.
₹120 for a cab.
You might make all these payments without thinking much about them.
But at the end of the day, do you know how much you actually spent?
What about the entire week or month?
Most people don't maintain an accurate record of their everyday spending.
Some people try using Excel sheets or notebooks, but eventually stop updating them because manually entering every transaction becomes tedious.
The real problem isn't spending money. It's not having clear visibility into where that money goes.
2. What is SpendWise?
SpendWise is designed to solve this problem by making expense recording and analysis easier.
Instead of manually maintaining an Excel sheet, users can quickly record their expenses through a simple interface.
The application will automatically:
Organize expenses into categories.
Calculate daily, weekly and monthly spending.
Show where most of the money is being spent.
Generate spending reports and visual charts.
Identify spending patterns.
Remind users to review their daily expenses.
The ultimate goal is to make expense tracking something that takes seconds rather than minutes.

6. Why are we creating SpendWise?
There are three main reasons.
1. Solve a real-life problem
People spend money every day but often don't maintain an accurate record of it. SpendWise makes tracking easier.
2. Reduce manual effort
Instead of maintaining Excel sheets, users can record transactions through a quick entry interface and let the application handle calculations, categorization and reporting.
3. Understand personal spending habits
Users can identify how much they spend on transportation, food, shopping, bills and other activities. This helps them make better financial decisions.
7. What is the long-term vision?
Initially, SpendWise will be a simple expense tracker.
But later, it could evolve into a personal finance intelligence application.
For example:
Detect unusual spending patterns.
Predict monthly expenses.
Compare spending across months.
Recommend realistic budgets.
Identify recurring subscriptions.
Generate personalized financial insights.
Support multiple transaction sources.
These are future possibilities, not requirements for the first version.
